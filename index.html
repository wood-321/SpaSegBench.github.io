About SpaSegBench

Quality measurements come from Supplementary Tables S2 and S3. SpaSegCompass also uses the supplied resource summary and dataset inventory to suggest whole-cell segmentation candidates.

Reading the evidence

  • Rankings are exploratory. Image and transcript / multimodal tracks are evaluated separately; dataset labels are not confirmed independent specimens.
  • Only metrics measured for every method in the same track are shown. Dataset coverage can differ, so a leading rank is not proof of general superiority.
  • Detection F1 is reported for inspection and receives no separate weight. Prior and reference status are assessed per metric; reference-concordant measurements are excluded from accuracy utility.
  • Flagged or quarantined values remain traceable in source records and are excluded from aggregation as defined by their status.
  • mDA averages TP / (TP + FP + FN) across IoU thresholds. It is not AP50 or confidence-ranked average precision.
  • Efficiency has no weight in the quality score. Runtime, task RAM and per-card VRAM screen historical runs; they do not predict new workloads.

Method versions, ROI-level quality–resource links and independent validation remain incomplete. Missing-evidence bounds are not confidence intervals. English source-note translations are provided for reading; original records remain available to download.
